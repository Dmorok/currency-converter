# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 3 October 2026_

**Currency Converter** does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

## What the extension stores

These are kept only in `chrome.storage.local` on your device:

- your currency list, main and home currency, last amount
- your settings (language, theme, rate source, decimals, fee %)
- the most recently downloaded exchange rates, so the extension works offline

None of this leaves your browser. Removing the extension deletes it.

## Network requests

The extension only sends plain `GET` requests for public exchange-rate data to:

- `cdn.moneyconvert.net`
- `cdn.jsdelivr.net` and `latest.currency-api.pages.dev`
- `open.er-api.com`
- `bank.gov.ua`

These requests contain no identifiers, cookies or information about you or the pages you visit. The rate providers may log standard request data (such as IP address) under their own policies.

## No tracking

There is no analytics, telemetry, advertising, account system or remote code.
